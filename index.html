About Our Department

The Department of Computer Science at Islamia University of Bahawalpur delivers modern education in software development, artificial intelligence, and data analytics. Our programs align with industry needs.

Students work in advanced computer labs with latest tools and technologies. We emphasize practical learning through projects and internships with top tech companies.

Our graduates secure positions at leading organizations worldwide. We focus on developing problem-solving skills and technical expertise for successful careers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum percentage required for admission?

Minimum 60% in Intermediate with Mathematics. FSc Pre-Engineering or ICS students are eligible.

Do you offer merit scholarships?

Yes, IUB offers merit scholarships to top 3 students of each semester. Need-based financial aid also available.

Is hostel facility available?

Yes, separate hostels for male and female students with mess, WiFi, and 24/7 security.

What is the class timing?

Morning classes from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Evening classes available for self-support programs.

Do you provide internship opportunities?

Yes, partnerships with 50+ software houses and IT companies for internships.
